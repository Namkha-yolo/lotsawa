Overview

Lotsawa Keyboard is a Tibetan transliteration keyboard that converts romanized input to Tibetan script. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your data.

Data Collection

We do not collect any data.

  • We do not collect or store any text you type
  • We do not track your keystrokes
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, location, or any other personal data
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools
  • We do not collect device identifiers

How the Keyboard Works

All text processing happens entirely on your device:

  • When you type romanized text, it is converted to Tibetan script locally
  • The dictionary and prediction data are stored on your device
  • No text ever leaves your device

Network Access

Lotsawa Keyboard works completely offline. The app:

  • Does not require an internet connection
  • Does not make any network requests
  • Does not send data to any servers

Full Access Permission

Lotsawa Keyboard does not request "Full Access" permission. The keyboard operates in the most restricted mode possible, ensuring your privacy is protected.

Third Parties

We do not share any data with third parties because we do not collect any data in the first place.

Children's Privacy

Our app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to this privacy policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above.
